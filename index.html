01 / DRAZELON X1

THE FIRST FLAGSHIP CONCEPT.

DRAZELON X1 is a high-performance electric luxury 2+2 coupe concept. It combines an aggressive dragon-inspired identity with a low, wide aerodynamic architecture, premium cockpit and intelligent vehicle systems.

04 / VISION

POWER BEYOND LIMITS.

DRAZELON is a created automotive brand concept focused on performance, technology, luxury, safety and a recognizable visual language. The X1 is the first flagship concept intended to open a pathway toward real engineering and industrial development.

Brand Story

DRAZE is a creative interpretation of DRAGON + DRIVE. ELON is interpreted as ELEVATED + ON. Together: “The power of the dragon that always moves forward.” This is brand storytelling, not a historical linguistic origin or reference to Elon Musk.

Mission

Turn an original design vision into a technically validated automotive program through qualified engineering, manufacturing and technology partners.

Global Vision

Open the project to qualified partners across China, the United States, France, Europe, Japan, South Korea and other automotive markets.
